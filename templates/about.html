MediCare ML — System Overview

MediCare ML is an intelligent medical management system powered by a supervised Multi-Class Random Forest Disease Classifier. By analyzing patient-reported symptom constellations across 132 clinical indicators, the platform accurately evaluates probabilities among 41 distinct human diseases, estimates clinical confidence, ranks differential diagnoses, and refers patients to the appropriate medical department.

End-to-End Symptom Diagnostic Workflow

Below is the step-by-step pipeline from symptom selection to disease classification and specialist referral:

1
Symptom Selection (Client Layer):
User selects multiple observable symptoms from 7 organ system categories or uses 1-click viva presets.
2
Vectorization & Feature Mapping (ml_model.py):
The selected symptom list is mapped into a 132-dimension multi-hot binary vector ([1, 0, 1, 0, ...]) matching the model feature schema.
3
Random Forest Multi-Class Inference:
An ensemble of 100 decision trees queries the symptom pattern to compute posterior class probabilities across all 41 candidate diseases.
4
Differential Diagnoses & Clinical Triaging:
The system extracts the top-1 condition plus 2nd and 3rd differential diagnoses, pairs them with medical precautions, and determines the recommended specialist department.
5
Database Storage & Referral Link:
Results are committed into SQLite; the user can click "Book Appointment with this Specialist" to auto-populate the appointment desk.

College Viva & Oral Exam Guide

Single-disease models (like diabetes or heart disease) use continuous physiological biomarkers (e.g. glucose, cholesterol) for binary classification (Yes/No). In contrast, our symptom-based multi-disease model is a multi-class classifier that takes a multi-hot binary vector across 132 observable symptoms and outputs probabilities across 41 distinct medical conditions simultaneously.

We use a Random Forest Classifier with 100 decision trees. Random Forests excel at high-dimensional binary feature spaces (132 binary indicators), prevent individual tree overfitting through bagging and feature subsampling, and provide probabilistic class distributions via predict_proba() for differential diagnosis ranking.

In clinical medicine, several diseases share overlapping symptoms (e.g. fever, chills, and headache can appear in Malaria, Dengue, or Typhoid). Rather than outputting a single rigid label, our model calculates the top 3 differential diagnoses with probability match percentages, giving doctors and patients a realistic clinical view.
